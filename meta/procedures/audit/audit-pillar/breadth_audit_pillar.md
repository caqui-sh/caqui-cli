# Technical Audit Pillar: Domain Delineation (Breadth)

The **Domain Delineation (Breadth)** pillar evaluates the horizontal reach and completeness of the active scope or universe under review. It focuses on delineating perimeter boundaries and verifying containment within those limits.

* **Key Question**: *How much of the horizontal territory was fenced off, and was that perimeter perfectly enforced?*
