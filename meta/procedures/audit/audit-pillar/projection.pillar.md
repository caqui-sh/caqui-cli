# Technical Audit Pillar: Resolution & Projection

The **Resolution & Projection** pillar evaluates the zoom level, clarity, and readability of the target asset. It ensures the analytical perspective is correctly calibrated to the consumer's needs and that transitions between adjacent layers of resolution are seamless.

* **Key Question**: *At what zoom level are we looking, how clear is the picture, and can the target receiver interpret it?*
