# Technical Audit Pillar: Resolution & Projection

The **Resolution & Projection** pillar evaluates the detail resolution, clarity, and readability of the target asset. It ensures the analytical perspective is correctly calibrated to the consumer's needs and that transitions between adjacent layers of resolution are seamless.

* **Key Question**: *At what resolution level are we projecting, how clear is the picture, and can the target receiver interpret it?*
