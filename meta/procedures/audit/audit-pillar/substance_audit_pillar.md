# Technical Audit Pillar: Semantic Content (Substance)

The **Semantic Content (Substance)** pillar evaluates the meaning, logic, and factual accuracy of the target asset. It determines whether the information makes sense, reflects reality, and maps correctly to its intent.

* **Key Question**: *Does it make sense, reflect reality, and map correctly to its intent?*
