# Technical Audit Pillar: Structural Architecture (Blueprint)

The **Structural Architecture (Blueprint)** pillar evaluates the layout, rules, and logic governing how the information space of a target asset is constructed. It spans the entire physical geometry of the target asset, from surface compliance to deep architectural mechanics, independent of what the content means.

* **Key Question**: *Is the structural blueprint correct?*
