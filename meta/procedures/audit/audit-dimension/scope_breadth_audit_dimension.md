# Technical Audit Specification: Scope - Breadth (scope_breadth_audit_dimension.md)

This specification defines the rules for the **Scope** dimension. It audits the boundary definition of the target asset's evaluation universe, explicitly delineating its perimeter boundaries, answering: *Where exactly are the boundary lines of the target asset's evaluation universe drawn?*

This dimension exists under the [**Domain Delineation (Breadth)**](../audit-pillar/breadth_audit_pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Objective & Spectrum-based**](../audit-nature/objective_spectrum_audit_nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Scope (The Perimeter)**:
  - **Constraint**: The perimeter boundaries of the target asset's evaluation universe must be explicitly and clearly defined.
  - **Audit Focus**: Sets the exact boundary line of the audit.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: It does not evaluate what was caught inside or outside the perimeter, only where the fence itself is drawn.
