# Technical Audit Specification: Syntax & Formatting - Structure (syntax_structure_audit_dimension.md)

This specification defines the rules for the **Syntax & Formatting** dimension. It audits syntax, layout, and formatting rule compliance, ensuring the target asset mechanically aligns with macro document specifications, notation conventions, or structural template constraints, answering: *Does the target asset violate the foundational formatting constraints, notation rules, or structural requirements of the medium?*

This dimension exists under the [**Structural Architecture (Blueprint)**](../audit-pillar/structure_audit_pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Objective & Discrete (Zero-Tolerance)**](../audit-nature/objective_discrete_audit_nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Syntax & Formatting (Rule Compliance)**:
  - **Constraint**: The target asset must mechanically align with all macro document specifications, notation rules, and structural layout schemas of the target asset.
  - **Audit Focus**: Catches formatting deviations, structural omissions, and notation rule violations.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: Completely blind to whether a value makes logical sense, satisfies boundaries, or represents semantic truth. It checks only structural compliance.
