# Technical Audit Specification: Veracity - Substance (veracity_substance_audit_dimension.md)

This specification defines the rules for the **Veracity** dimension. It audits the factual accuracy of factual claims and quantitative values of the target asset, ensuring they align directly when cross-referenced against an objective source of truth, answering: *Are the ground-level factual claims and quantitative values of the target asset correct when cross-referenced with an objective source of truth?*

This dimension exists under the [**Semantic Content (Substance)**](../audit-pillar/substance_audit_pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Objective & Spectrum-based**](../audit-nature/objective_spectrum_audit_nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Veracity (Fact Accuracy)**:
  - **Constraint**: All ground-level factual claims and quantitative values within the target asset must be correct and directly aligned when cross-referenced with an objective source of truth.
  - **Audit Focus**: Validates ground-level factual claims of the target asset.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: Checks the raw facts at the ground level, completely independent of whatever overarching conceptual interpretations have been applied to them.
