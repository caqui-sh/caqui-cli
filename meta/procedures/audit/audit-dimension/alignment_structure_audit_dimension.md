# Technical Audit Specification: Alignment - Structure (alignment_structure_audit_dimension.md)

This specification defines the rules for the **Alignment** dimension. It audits internal logical alignment and consistency, ensuring that different components or layers of detail within the target asset assert consistent realities and do not contradict one another, answering: *Do separate components or layers of detail within the target asset assert clashing or mutually exclusive realities?*

This dimension exists under the [**Structural Architecture (Blueprint)**](../audit-pillar/structure_audit_pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Objective & Discrete (Zero-Tolerance)**](../audit-nature/objective_discrete_audit_nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Alignment (Internal Consistency)**:
  - **Constraint**: All cross-boundary assertions within the target asset must assert a consistent reality. Mutual contradictions are prohibited.
  - **Audit Focus**: Identifies and flags internal contradictions and clashing realities across the target asset.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: Focuses strictly on internal contradictions, regardless of whether the overall structure of the target asset is compliant with formatting constraints or if the claims correspond to the external truth.
