# Audit Dimension Specification *(Atomic & Terminal / Non-Divisible)*: Token Precision - Projection (token_precision.dimension.md)

This specification defines the rules for the **Token Precision** dimension. It audits the exactness and completeness of the target asset's technical details, answering: *Are all technical details and conditions specified with exactness and completeness, avoiding vague summaries or skipped steps?*

This dimension exists under the [**Resolution & Projection**](audit-pillar/projection.pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Subjective & Spectrum-based**](audit-nature/subjective_spectrum.nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Token Precision (Completeness & Exactness)**:
  - **Constraint**: All content within the target asset must be defined with technical exactness and informational completeness, avoiding vagueness or skipped operational details.
  - **Audit Focus**: Identifies and flags vague summaries, hand-waving explanations, or omitted parameters/conditions that are necessary to make the technical details clear.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: Focuses exclusively on the exactness of the signal. It does not measure how concisely the message is written (Token Brevity) or how easy it is for the receiver to parse (Reader Accessibility).
