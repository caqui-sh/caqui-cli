# Audit Dimension Specification *(Atomic & Terminal / Non-Divisible)*: Internal Alignment - Substance (internal_alignment.dimension.md)

This specification defines the rules for the **Internal Alignment** dimension. It audits internal logical consistency, ensuring that different components or layers of detail within the target asset assert consistent realities and do not contradict one another, answering: *Do separate components or layers of detail within the target asset assert clashing or mutually exclusive realities?*

This dimension exists under the [**Semantic Content (Substance)**](audit-pillar/substance.pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Objective & Discrete (Zero-Tolerance)**](audit-nature/objective_discrete.nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Internal Alignment (Logical Consistency)**:
  - **Constraint**: All cross-boundary assertions within the target asset must assert a consistent reality. Mutual contradictions are prohibited.
  - **Audit Focus**: Identifies and flags internal contradictions and clashing realities across the target asset.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: Focuses strictly on internal contradictions and semantic clashing. It is blind to external factual truth (Semantic Veracity) or structural formatting rules (Notation Conformance).
