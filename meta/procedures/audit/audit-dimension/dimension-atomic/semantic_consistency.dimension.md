# Audit Dimension Specification *(Atomic & Terminal)*: Semantic Consistency - Substance (semantic_consistency.dimension.md)

This specification defines the rules for the **Semantic Consistency** dimension. It audits internal logical and terminological consistency, ensuring that different components or layers of detail within the target asset assert consistent realities and use uniform expressions for the same concepts, answering: *Do separate components or layers of detail within the target asset assert clashing realities, or use fragmented expressions for identical concepts (minimizing semantic fragmentation / maximizing semantic consistency)?*

This dimension exists under the [**Semantic Content (Substance)**](audit-pillar/substance.pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Objective & Discrete (Zero-Tolerance)**](audit-nature/objective_discrete.nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Semantic Consistency (Logical & Terminological Consistency)**:
  - **Constraint**: All cross-boundary assertions and concept expressions within the target asset must be logically consistent and terminologically uniform. Mutual contradictions and fragmented naming/phrasing are prohibited.
  - **Audit Focus**: Identifies and flags internal logical contradictions, clashing realities, and inconsistent conceptual terminology across the target asset.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: Focuses strictly on internal logical and terminological agreement within the target asset (preventing contradictions and fragmentation). It is blind to whether assertions are correct relative to reality (Semantic Veracity), whether the conceptual model itself is accurate (Semantic Fidelity), or whether the asset provides a diverse variety of conceptual representations (Semantic Diversity).
