# Audit Dimension Specification *(Atomic & Terminal)*: Depth Continuity - Projection (depth_continuity.dimension.md)

This specification defines the rules for the **Depth Continuity** dimension. It audits the macro-level progression and gradient transition between adjacent abstraction layers, ensuring that the structural flow of abstraction and reasoning is continuous across the hierarchy to prevent abrupt leaps in reasoning or perspective resolution, answering: *Is the macro-level progression across abstraction layers continuous and logically step-wise, preventing perspective leaps and transition friction (minimizing perspective leaps and transition friction / maximizing reasoning continuity)?*

This dimension exists under the [**Resolution & Projection**](audit-pillar/projection.pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Subjective & Spectrum-based**](audit-nature/subjective_spectrum.nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Depth Continuity (Abstraction Gradient)**:
  - **Constraint**: The macro-level transition between adjacent layers of abstraction must be continuous and logical, establishing clear intermediate context to avoid perspective leaps and transition friction.
  - **Audit Focus**: Identifies and flags perspective leaps and transition friction, where sections transition between highly abstract concepts and granular specifications without providing the necessary intermediate structural layers or context.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: Focuses exclusively on the transition gradient between adjacent layers (auditing perspective leaps and transition friction). It is blind to the definition of the global hierarchy itself (Perspective Depth) or localized leaf-level detail density (Depth Conformance).
