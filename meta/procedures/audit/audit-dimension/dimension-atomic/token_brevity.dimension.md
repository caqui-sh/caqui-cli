# Audit Dimension Specification *(Atomic & Terminal / Non-Divisible)*: Token Brevity - Projection (token_brevity.dimension.md)

This specification defines the rules for the **Token Brevity** dimension. It audits the conciseness of the target asset, answering: *Is the target asset free of wordy phrasing, fluff, and unnecessary boilerplate (maximizing conciseness)?*

This dimension exists under the [**Resolution & Projection**](audit-pillar/projection.pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Subjective & Spectrum-based**](audit-nature/subjective_spectrum.nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Token Brevity (Conciseness)**:
  - **Constraint**: The target asset must convey its information using the minimum necessary number of words or elements, eliminating wordiness and fluff.
  - **Audit Focus**: Identifies and flags wordy phrasing, unnecessary boilerplate, repetitive descriptions, and non-functional verbiage.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: Focuses on signal conciseness. It does not measure whether the content has lost vital technical details (Token Precision) or whether the vocabulary is accessible (Reader Accessibility).
