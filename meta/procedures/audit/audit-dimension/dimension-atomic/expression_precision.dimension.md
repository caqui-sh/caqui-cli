# Audit Dimension Specification *(Atomic & Terminal)*: Expression Precision - Projection (expression_precision.dimension.md)

This specification defines the rules for the **Expression Precision** dimension. It audits the exactness and specificity of the representation, answering: *Does the target asset describe details with exactness, avoiding vague generalizations (minimizing representation approximation / maximizing detail specificity)?*

This dimension exists under the [**Resolution & Projection**](audit-pillar/projection.pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Subjective & Spectrum-based**](audit-nature/subjective_spectrum.nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Expression Precision (Completeness & Exactness)**:
  - **Constraint**: Convey the semantic signal with exactness, avoiding vague generalizations or conceptual approximations.
  - **Audit Focus**: Evaluates the representation to identify coarse approximations or non-specific generalities that obscure the semantic signal.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: Focuses purely on maximizing detail specificity, independent of representation volume (Expression Brevity) or readability (Reader Accessibility).
