# Audit Dimension Specification *(Atomic & Terminal)*: Expression Precision - Projection (expression_precision.dimension.md)

This specification audits target asset detail preservation, answering: *Does the target asset represent the semantic signal while minimizing the loss of detail (minimizing loss of detail / maximizing detail preservation)?*

This dimension exists under the [**Resolution & Projection**](audit-pillar/projection.pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Subjective & Spectrum-based**](audit-nature/subjective_spectrum.nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Expression Precision (Completeness & Exactness)**:
  - **Constraint**: Convey the semantic signal while minimizing the loss of detail.
  - **Audit Focus**: Evaluates the representation to identify any loss of detail that obscures the semantic signal.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: Focuses purely on minimizing the loss of detail, independent of representation volume (Expression Brevity) or readability (Reader Accessibility).
