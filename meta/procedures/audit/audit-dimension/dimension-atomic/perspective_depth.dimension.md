# Audit Dimension Specification *(Atomic & Terminal)*: Perspective Depth - Projection (perspective_depth.dimension.md)

This specification defines the rules for the **Perspective Depth** dimension. It audits the global, hierarchical layers of abstraction and perspective of the target asset, answering: *Is the global vertical hierarchy of perspective depth levels correctly calibrated to the objective (minimizing perspective misalignment / maximizing perspective calibration)?*

This dimension exists under the [**Resolution & Projection**](audit-pillar/projection.pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Subjective & Spectrum-based**](audit-nature/subjective_spectrum.nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Perspective Depth (Hierarchical Abstraction Layers)**:
  - **Constraint**: The overall perspective depth levels of the target asset must be nested logically and calibrated to the target objective.
  - **Audit Focus**: Verifies that the nested hierarchy of perspective depth levels is structurally coherent, does not skip steps in the cascade, and avoids abrupt perspective mismatches between parent and child elements.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: Focuses on defining and calibrating the global hierarchy of perspective levels. It does not audit localized leaf-level detail density (Depth Conformance) or transition gradients between adjacent layers (Depth Continuity).
