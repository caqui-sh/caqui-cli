# Audit Dimension Specification *(Atomic & Terminal)*: Semantic Veracity - Substance (semantic_veracity.dimension.md)

This specification defines the rules for the **Semantic Veracity** dimension. It audits the factual accuracy of factual claims and quantitative values of the target asset, ensuring they align directly when cross-referenced against an objective source of truth, answering: *Are the ground-level factual claims and quantitative values of the target asset correct when cross-referenced with an objective source of truth (minimizing factual deviation / maximizing ground-truth alignment)?*

This dimension exists under the [**Semantic Content (Substance)**](audit-pillar/substance.pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Objective & Spectrum-based**](audit-nature/objective_spectrum.nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Semantic Veracity (Fact Accuracy)**:
  - **Constraint**: All ground-level factual claims and quantitative values within the target asset must be correct and directly aligned when cross-referenced with an objective source of truth.
  - **Audit Focus**: Validates ground-level factual claims of the target asset.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: Focuses strictly on the correctness of factual claims relative to external reality. It is blind to whether the target asset is internally self-contradictory (Semantic Consistency), whether the underlying conceptual model is accurate (Semantic Fidelity), or whether concepts are represented from multiple angles (Semantic Diversity).
