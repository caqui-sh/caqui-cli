# Audit Dimension Specification *(Atomic & Terminal)*: Semantic Diversity - Substance (semantic_diversity.dimension.md)

This specification defines the rules for the **Semantic Diversity** dimension. It audits target asset conceptual richness and multi-angled representation, ensuring complex concepts are presented with sufficient variety to enhance comprehension and prevent design fixation, answering: *Does the target asset describe complex concepts from multiple, diverse angles to ensure complete understanding (minimizing conceptual rigidity / maximizing representation diversity)?*

This dimension exists under the [**Semantic Content (Substance)**](audit-pillar/substance.pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Subjective & Spectrum-based**](audit-nature/subjective_spectrum.nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Semantic Diversity (Representation Richness)**:
  - **Constraint**: Complex or central semantic concepts must be represented from a diverse range of accurate angles or conceptual frameworks where variety is necessary for complete cognitive capture.
  - **Audit Focus**: Evaluates the representation of central concepts to identify monochrome framing or conceptual rigidity that limits understanding or invites design fixation.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: Focuses strictly on the richness and variety of conceptual representation (providing multiple accurate framing perspectives). It is blind to whether those representations contain internal logical or terminological contradictions (Semantic Consistency), whether they are factually correct (Semantic Veracity), or whether the overarching conceptual model matches reality (Semantic Fidelity).
