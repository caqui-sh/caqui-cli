# Audit Dimension Specification *(Atomic & Terminal)*: Notation Conformance - Structure (notation_conformance.dimension.md)

This specification defines the rules for the **Notation Conformance** dimension (formerly Syntax & Formatting). It audits inline representation, syntax, and mechanical notation rules, answering: *Does the target asset violate any character-level or line-level formatting constraints, notation conventions, or mechanical syntax rules (minimizing syntax infractions / maximizing style conformance)?*

This dimension exists under the [**Structural Architecture (Blueprint)**](audit-pillar/structure.pillar.md) pillar.

This dimension is [**Objective & Discrete (Zero-Tolerance)**](audit-nature/objective_discrete.nature.md) in nature.

---

## Dimension Constraints

* **Notation Conformance (Terminal Syntax & Style Compliance)**:
  - **Constraint**: The mechanical syntax, characters, spacing, and cosmetic layout must comply with the defined notation standards.
  - **Asymmetric & Terminal Nature**: Notation Conformance is terminal and non-layered, operating at the leaf level of physical notation and syntax representation. It inherits the container framework established by Macro Structure and copy-edits the leaf contents.
  - **Audit Focus**: Catches character-level syntax errors, mechanical spacing violations, invalid inline elements, and general cosmetic compliance failures.
  - **Mutual Exclusive Distinction**: Focuses exclusively on micro-level compliance. It is blind to the global heading layout (Macro Structure) and semantic/logical correctness of the content.
