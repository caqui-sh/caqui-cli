# Technical Audit Nature: Objective & Discrete (Zero-Tolerance)

This dimension is objective and discrete in nature. Rather than a simple binary grade, it operates on a zero-tolerance model: it audits the target asset for specific, countable infractions that are individually cataloged, where a 'pass' is defined strictly as the complete absence of any such discrete violations.

* **Evaluation Profile**: Objective / Discrete (Zero-Tolerance)
