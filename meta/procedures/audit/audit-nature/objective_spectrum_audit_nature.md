# Technical Audit Nature: Objective & Spectrum-based

This dimension is objective but spectrum-based, evaluated against clear external reference criteria where compliance exists on a continuous scale of coverage, precision, or degree rather than a binary state.

* **Evaluation Profile**: Objective / Spectrum
